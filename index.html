How it works

Paste any YouTube video or playlist link and it joins the queue. When one video ends, the next one starts on its own.

Links that work

  • youtube.com/watch?v=... links
  • youtu.be short links, Shorts, and live links
  • Playlist links (the whole playlist plays as one queue entry)

Links that don't

  • Channel pages. YouTube doesn't let a website list a channel's videos without an API key.
  • Videos whose owners turned off embedding. Watchlist shows a link to open them on YouTube, then moves on.

Your data

The queue and library are saved in your own browser only. Nothing is sent to a server.

Videos are played by YouTube's own embedded player.
